# Credits

## Demo track

`public/audio/track.mp3` was generated in 2026 with **YuE2**, the open music model from Multimodal
Art Projection (M·A·P) and HKUST. The `element`, `decode` and `file` entry points play it; the
`stream` entry plays the synthesized loop in `server/synth.ts` instead.

## YuE2 licence

- **Inference code, agent skill and docs:** Apache 2.0.
- **Model weights (`YuE2-3B`, `YuE2-Vae`):** CC BY-NC 4.0, plus an **additional individual creator
  permission dated 2026-09-16**.

The creator permission lets personal users, content creators and musicians acting in an individual
capacity generate outputs and *"publish, distribute, sell, license or otherwise monetize those
outputs"*, with no fees or royalties payable to the model licensors, subject to responsible-use
conditions (no illegal, harmful, deceptive or unethical use). Companies that want to use the weights
commercially need a separate licence; the weights themselves stay CC BY-NC 4.0 and may not be
redistributed or sold commercially.

For the track in this repository:

- It is published and redistributed in a free, public demo; the creator permission allows this,
  including on commercial platforms.
- It is credited to YuE2; the permission makes credit optional.
- The permission covers only rights held by the model licensors, and grants nothing in third-party
  material used as input (someone else's lyrics, melody or recording).
- Copyright in AI-generated material varies by jurisdiction and may not subsist at all (the US
  Copyright Office requires human authorship); the track is used under a permission, not claimed as
  an exclusive asset.

The track is not covered by this repository's MIT licence.
